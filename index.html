How to read this tablePrice · Volume · Moving averages · Cap

Price

Last = latest Yahoo quote (local ccy; ~15 min delayed). Grey = quote time (Geneva) while open, else last close date. Price returns only (no dividends). Name colour = today's move (up / down). As of ….

Volume

Ratio = last ÷ N-day average (5/20/50). Last = today so far while a market is open. 1.0× = average day; > 1.5× highlighted. Grey figure = N-day avg volume.

Moving averages & cap

% vs N-day SMA: +% above , −% below . Mkt cap / EV in EUR (Yahoo; FX as of …). Yields in bp.

News

    Headlines via Google News RSS and official exchange feeds (Oslo Børs NewsWeb, Nasdaq Nordic). Links open the publisher in a new tab. Times in Geneva time.

    Markets
    Table: Yahoo Finance quotes and daily data via GitHub Actions (prices every 15 min in market hours). Nordic Power: EEX settlements, ENTSO-E Transparency day-ahead prices, SE4 wind/load/outages and reservoirs (Energy-Charts / Energi Data Service fallback), NVE hydro, Svenska kraftnät SE4 load/flows. Charts: TradingView widgets; exchanges TradingView won't embed for free (London, Hong Kong, TVC indices) get a simple chart drawn here from CNBC's delayed price bars. Treasury yields cannot be embedded, so the Rates chart uses bond ETFs and Table yield rows open a Yahoo 1Y chart. Markets search uses a static symbol index (LSE/AIM, OMX Stockholm, Oslo, NYSE/Nasdaq, TSX/TSXV, HKEX; works offline). TradingView lookup is tried when reachable; exotic names may need EXCH:SYM or in-chart search.
