How to read this tablePrice · Volume · Moving averages

Price

Last is the latest daily close from Yahoo Finance (delayed, end-of-day; tagged live if a session was still open when fetched), in local currency. Market is the listing venue. Data refreshes twice each weekday (before the European open and after the US close); as of …. Returns are price-only, excluding dividends.

Volume

Ratio = last session's volume ÷ average daily volume of the prior N sessions (5, 10, 20, 50 days). 1.0× = an average day; above 1.5× is highlighted as unusually heavy trading. The small grey figure is the N-day average itself.

Moving averages

% distance of the last price from its N-day simple moving average: +4.0% = 4% above the MA , −4.0% = 4% below . Yields are shown in basis points.

News

    Headlines via Google News RSS and official exchange feeds (Oslo Børs NewsWeb, Nasdaq Nordic). Links open the publisher in a new tab. Times in Geneva time.

    Markets
    Table: Yahoo Finance daily data via GitHub Actions. Nordic Power: EEX settlements, Energy-Charts (CC BY 4.0) day-ahead prices, NVE hydro. Charts: TradingView widgets. Treasury yields cannot be embedded, so Monitor Rates uses bond ETFs and Table yield rows open a Yahoo 1Y chart. Markets search uses a static symbol index (LSE/AIM, OMX Stockholm, Oslo, NYSE/Nasdaq, TSX/TSXV, HKEX; works offline). TradingView lookup is tried when reachable; exotic names may need EXCH:SYM or in-chart search.
