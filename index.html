How to read this tablePrice · Volume · Moving averages · Cap

Price

Last = Yahoo daily close (local ccy; delayed ~15 min). Price returns only (no dividends). As of ….

Volume

Ratio = last ÷ N-day average (5/20/50). 1.0× = average day; > 1.5× highlighted. Grey figure = N-day avg volume.

Moving averages & cap

% vs N-day SMA: +% above , −% below . Mkt cap / EV in EUR (Yahoo; FX as of …). Yields in bp.

News

    Headlines via Google News RSS and official exchange feeds (Oslo Børs NewsWeb, Nasdaq Nordic). Links open the publisher in a new tab. Times in Geneva time.

    Markets
    Table: Yahoo Finance daily data via GitHub Actions. Nordic Power: EEX settlements, Energy-Charts (CC BY 4.0) day-ahead prices, NVE hydro, Svenska kraftnät SE4 load/flows (ENTSO-E when enabled). Charts: TradingView widgets. Treasury yields cannot be embedded, so Monitor Rates uses bond ETFs and Table yield rows open a Yahoo 1Y chart. Markets search uses a static symbol index (LSE/AIM, OMX Stockholm, Oslo, NYSE/Nasdaq, TSX/TSXV, HKEX; works offline). TradingView lookup is tried when reachable; exotic names may need EXCH:SYM or in-chart search.
